# T.S. Mishra University Website Redesign

## Goal
Replace the starter screen with a polished, public-facing university website, grounded in information verified from TSM’s official website.

## Work
- Build a cinematic, responsive homepage with editorial university storytelling, official schools and programs, campus life, hospital information, recent news, contact details, and admission calls to action.
- Add dedicated About, Academics, Admissions, Administration, Student Life, Hospital, News & Events, and Contact pages with clear navigation and distinct page layouts.
- Use TSM-hosted photography and logo assets where they can be verified; never present invented imagery or unverified facts as official. Link to the official TSM site for pages or details not confirmed for this redesign.
- Add accessible responsive navigation, working internal/external links, validated contact/enquiry forms with honest non-submission success messaging, reduced-motion-aware animation, and page-specific SEO metadata.
- Apply a consistent navy, warm-white editorial design system and verify the main flows, routes, and viewport behavior.

## Technical approach
- Keep the existing TanStack Start and Tailwind v4 stack; use file-based TanStack routes and shared frontend components/styles.
- No backend, authentication, or claim of saved/delivered form submissions.
- Maintain a concise root task roadmap and record the routing/component architecture decision in `AGENTS.md`.

## Verified content boundaries
- Official site identifies eight schools: Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence, Allied and Healthcare Sciences, Business Management, Law, and Ayurveda.
- Official site lists 90+ acres, 2,000+ students, and hospital figures; use these only with attribution to the official source and avoid reinterpreting dated/conflicting metrics.
- Official admissions and contact details will be copied only from the official TSM site. Unverified leadership, accreditation, testimonials, awards, or statistics will be omitted or clearly marked for confirmation.
